## Decisions

- Permobil AS: the captured copyright identifies only the Permobil brand. The captured homepage, contact, privacy and terms pages do not supply the Norwegian company orgnr, registered email firmapost@permobil.com, phone 67 07 04 00 or Grorudveien 55. Withhold as ambiguous, reason **group website**. The review does not claim that a Permobil AB footer was found in these captured pages; that variant is covered by a regression test.
- Gravitonia AS: the .com site identifies the Gravitonia game/brand and copyright but no Norwegian legal entity or registered corroboration. Withhold under the same rule; this is uncertainty, not a claim that a particular foreign owner was proved.
- Quickminds AS: the saved About page explicitly identifies Quickminds AS and its founding. Retain; a Norwegian entity is present beyond the short brand. The collector retains that page as supporting evidence so the final/carry-forward guard reaches the same decision.
- The remaining 22 domains are .no and remain under their existing proof rules.

The guard applies at collection and again at final publication, covering carried-forward snapshots and suppressing derived company-site claims. It preserves the existing stronger owner-conflict rules. Registered contact/address or exact orgnr on any fetched site page can corroborate the Norwegian company. No company allowlist or special case was added.
