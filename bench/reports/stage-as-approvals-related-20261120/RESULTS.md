# Stage AS: bundled approvals and related-company websites

Baseline: 87f256eec34c05adb1f05e84c68c0d3a7c7bd91c. No push.

## Changes and scope

Three deterministic official selected-row gzip indexes; manifests retain official URL, retrieval time, raw dataset hash, row count and source generation timestamp (null when absent). No raw downloads are committed. Official BRREG full CSV is reused from its recorded download, not fetched again. Approval files were downloaded once for this build.

Exact main-unit identifiers only. Cleaning publishes literal status and a strictly mapped approved flag. Workshops retain approval number, type and location, with no subunit-to-parent reassignment. Related-company websites require the existing published registry relationship plus the target registry row and cite both. They are explicitly registry-declared, not ownership-verified. All original claims are asserted identical in replay; no official_website, social, brand, news or veto logic changes.
