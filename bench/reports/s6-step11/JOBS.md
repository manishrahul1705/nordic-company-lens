# Company-site / ATS jobs

38 nominated URLs were checked across 21 companies; zero new individual ACTIVE postings passed. The three NAV jobs remain. This is a recall limitation, not evidence that those employers have no vacancies. Conplan individual-looking career pages and Laksesystemer service-technician pages were fetched, but the current parser requires a structured JobPosting with supported posting/expiry dates. It does not infer active status from an undated careers page or a landing page. Some nominations are ordinary pages or article text containing career terms; none was published as a job. No linked external ATS board produced a new proved posting in this sample. Tenant-boundary and employer-mismatch cases are covered by synthetic end-to-end tests.
