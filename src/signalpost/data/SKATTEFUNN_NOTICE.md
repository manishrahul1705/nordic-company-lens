# SkatteFUNN historical approval snapshot

Attribution: Forskningsrådet, [NLOD 2.0](https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0).

The catalogue lists “Innsendte søknader til SkatteFUNN” under NLOD 2.0:
https://data.norge.no/nb/datasets/f82d3d4a-b0f8-3220-83ef-15fbd854c54f/innsendte-soknader-til-skattefunn

Its nov24.xlsx distribution returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-20. This index uses the provider's current publication of the same dataset, linked from https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/suksesshistorier/, and treats it as covered by the dataset's catalogued licence:
https://www.forskningsradet.no/siteassets/skattefunn/tall/skattefunn-innsendte-soknader-2002-2024.xlsx

The manifest records the complete provenance, retrieval timestamp, hashes, row counts and transformations. Only selected cells from approved, exact-orgnr, unambiguous project rows are retained. Evidence extracts identify their original sheet and row and the full workbook hash. No raw workbook is distributed. Missing dates are not invented. SkatteFUNN is an R&D tax deduction scheme; approval is not a grant, cash award or proof of a claimed deduction. The index is historical and incomplete for later applications.
